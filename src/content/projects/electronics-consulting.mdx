## Context

Hardware products often stall quietly — in bring-up, power budgets, radio and firmware integration, or the stretch between a working prototype and something ready for manufacturing. Those gaps are where schedules slip and surprises compound.

## Role & scope

Technical consulting across electronics design review, embedded architecture, prototype bring-up, and scoped implementation alongside your team when that is the fastest path forward.

## Approach

Engagements stay concrete: review the design, name the risks, propose a path, and where useful, implement. The work draws on the same kinds of problems seen across Hnykkur projects — MCU and sensor pipelines, BLE product paths, power and environmental constraints, PCB and enclosure iteration, and firmware that has to survive outside the lab.

## Outcome

Ongoing consulting support for companies and teams developing electronics-driven products, with an emphasis on fewer surprises between prototype and production.
