## Context

Hardware products fail quietly — in bring-up, in power budgets, in the gap between schematic and firmware, or in the last stretch before manufacturing. Consulting engagements focus on those friction points.

## Role & scope

Technical consulting across electronics design review, embedded architecture, prototype bring-up, and product development decisions.

## Approach

Engagements are concrete and scoped: review the design, identify risk, propose a path, and where useful, implement alongside your team. The goal is fewer surprises between prototype and production.

## Outcome

Ongoing consulting support for companies and teams developing electronics-driven products.
