## Context

Hanzi Puzzle explores how learners can build intuition for Chinese characters through structured play — not flashcards, but spatial and compositional puzzles grounded in how characters are written and remembered.

## Role & scope

Product concept, interaction design, and software implementation — from early prototypes through iterative refinement of the learning loop.

## Approach

The work centers on decomposing characters into meaningful parts, then reassembling them through puzzles that reward attention rather than rote repetition. The product prioritizes clarity, calm UI, and a learning cadence that feels deliberate.

## Outcome

An ongoing product in active development, refining puzzle mechanics and the learning experience based on continued use and iteration.
