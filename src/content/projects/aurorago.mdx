## Context

Seeing the aurora depends on space weather, cloud cover, darkness, and location. AuroraGo brings those signals together so people in Iceland — and visitors — can decide when conditions are worth going out for.

## Role & scope

Product and software development spanning forecast presentation, location-aware guidance, and a focused mobile experience designed for cold, dark nights outdoors.

## Approach

The product emphasizes signal over noise: clear forecasts, practical timing, and an interface that works when you are away from a desk. Engineering choices favor reliability and readability over feature sprawl.

## Outcome

A shipped mobile experience used to plan aurora outings under real Icelandic conditions.
