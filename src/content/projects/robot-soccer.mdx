## Context

Robot soccer is a demanding proving ground for autonomous systems — tight timing, noisy sensing, physical contact, and decisions that must happen on the field, not in a lab after the fact.

## Role & scope

Embedded systems and control work across sensing, actuation, on-robot software, and the integration needed for coordinated play.

## Approach

The engineering focus is reliable loops: perception that holds under motion, control that stays stable under disturbance, and software architecture that can be iterated between matches without becoming fragile.

## Outcome

An ongoing robotics programme advancing autonomous play through embedded systems and field-tested iteration.
