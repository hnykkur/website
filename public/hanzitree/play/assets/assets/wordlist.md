# Course vocabulary & components

These two skill-tree nodes share the **same course wordlist** (`wordlist.tsv`): they differ only in **puzzle style**.

## Vocabulary (bottom right)

- Build the target character from **IDS components** using the diagram and buttons.
- Progress follows your **unlocked course level** (sections / units in `LevelStructure.txt`).

## Components (bottom left)

- Similar pool and leveling, with emphasis on **component-level** guessing (same underlying rows; different presentation rules in the app).

## Scope

Use **Settings** to limit **section / unit** or play the full unlocked span. The small **debug line** on the card can show which course line you are on.
