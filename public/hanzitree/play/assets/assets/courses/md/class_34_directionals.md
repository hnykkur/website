# Name the place, then the side

Chinese says the landmark first, then **front / back / beside / in / on**. English usually does the reverse. This lesson is choosing **前面**, **后面**, **旁边**, **外面**, **里面**, **上面**, or **下面**.

## Around a place

**前面** in front of. **后面** behind. **旁边** next to. **外面** outside.

**银行在学校前面。** The bank is in front of the school.

**咖啡馆在公园旁边。** The café is next to the park.

## In, on, under

**里面** inside. **上面** on. **下面** under.

**书在包里面。** The book is inside the bag.

**猫在桌子下面。** The cat is under the table.

Short **里 / 外 / 上 / 下** mean the same thing; this set uses the two-character forms. The English line on each card is the clue.
