# Component pictographs

**Component pictographs** teaches **IDS building blocks** that often appear as **leaves** rather than standalone words: walking (辶, 廴), enclosure (凵), sprout (屮), claw (爫), cloth foot (疋), and related forms.

## Why a separate set

These glyphs power compounds like **过**, **这**, **出**, and **艸** — recognizing them speeds up **foundations** and **vocabulary** assembly later.

## Mode

**Pictographic** recognition — whole-glyph choice, not assembly.
