# People at home

Assemble one character from its parts. In a two-character word the rest of the word stays visible.

Parents and siblings often double: **爸爸**, **哥哥**. **儿子 / 女儿** share **儿**. Partners are **老公 / 老婆**. People around you are **朋友**, **同学**, **孩子**, **室友**, **邻居**, **同事**.

**有** is “to have.” Counting people in a family uses **口** (**几口人**).
