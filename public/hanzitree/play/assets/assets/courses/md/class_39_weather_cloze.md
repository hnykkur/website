# How the weather is

Fill the missing word. English tells you which. Most blanks are Weather words; a few are **有**, **会**, **又**, **已经**, **好像**, or **突然**.

**今晚有暴风雨。** Tonight there will be a storm. (**暴风雨**)
**今天下午是晴天。** This afternoon is sunny. (**晴天**)
**突然下雨了。** It suddenly rained. (**突然**)

Thirty cards. Time words stay in the sentence; one blank each.
