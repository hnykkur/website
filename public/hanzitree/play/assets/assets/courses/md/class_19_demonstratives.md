# This, that, these, those

Near or far, one or many. This lesson is choosing **这个**, **那个**, **这些**, or **那些**.

## One: 个 comes along

**这个** this. **那个** that. The **个** is the measure word, already built in.

**那个床很重。** That bed is heavy.

## Many: no extra measure word

**这些** these. **那些** those. They take a noun, not **个**.

**这些杯子很大。** These cups are big.

English on the card marks this/that and singular/plural so only one fits. Fill the blank with the demonstrative.
