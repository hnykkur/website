# How you feel

Fill the missing word. English tells you which. Most blanks are Feelings words; a few are **特别**, **比较**, **总是**, **有些**, **更加**, or **几乎**.

**我很高兴。** I am happy. (**高兴**)
**我特别累。** I am especially tired. (**特别**)
**我几乎睡着了。** I almost fell asleep. (**几乎**)

Thirty cards. Mixed people and situations; one blank each.
