# Three ways to say “not”

Chinese splits English *not* into three small words. This lesson is choosing among **不**, **没**, and **别**.

## 不 — generally not

**不** denies a habit, an identity, or how something is — not a finished event.

**我不喜欢咖啡。** I don't like coffee.

## 没 — it didn't happen

**没** means the action never took place. Say **没** + verb, and leave **了** off.

**我没看电影。** I didn't watch a movie.

## 别 — don't (a command)

**别** tells someone not to do something: stop, or don't start.

**别走！** Don't leave!

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill the blank with **不**, **没**, or **别**.
