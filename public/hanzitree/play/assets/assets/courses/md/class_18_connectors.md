# And, or, than

These words join two things. This lesson is choosing among **和**, **还是**, **比**, **没有**, and **跟**.

## 和 / 跟 / 还是

**和** is *and*. **跟** is *with* a person. **还是** is *or* in a choice question.

**我喜欢咖啡和茶。** I like coffee and tea.

**我跟你去医院。** I'll go to the hospital with you.

**你喜欢游泳还是跑步？** Do you like swimming or running?

## 比 / 没有

**比** means more [adjective] than. **没有** here means *not as … as* — not “there isn't,” and not the past *didn't*.

**书房比卧室大。** The study is bigger than the bedroom.

**咖啡没有茶好喝。** Coffee is not as tasty as tea.

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill the blank with the joining word.
