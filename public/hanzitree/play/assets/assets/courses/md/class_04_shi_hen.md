# Naming, describing, “the most”

English *is* splits into three jobs here. This lesson is choosing **是**, **很**, or **最**.

## 是 — this is a noun

**是** names what someone or something *is* — a noun, not an adjective.

**他是老师。** He is a teacher.

## 很 — before an adjective

Adjectives don't need **是**. Neutral sentences put **很** in front. Here **很** often means *is*, not *very*.

**她很好。** She is well.

## 最 — the most

**最** goes in front of the adjective (or a feeling verb) for *most* / *-est*. The adjective itself does not grow an ending.

**这个最贵。** This is the most expensive.

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill the blank with **是**, **很**, or **最**.
