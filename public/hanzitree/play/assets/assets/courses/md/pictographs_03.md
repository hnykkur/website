# Body pictographs

This lesson covers **body and life** pictographs: face, heart, limbs, animals, fire, wood, water, and related **simple forms**.

## How to use this node

**Pictographic** mode is **recognition only** — pick the Hanzi that matches the English clue. There is no component assembly here.

## Why these shapes stick

These glyphs are **frequent building blocks** inside longer words (心 in 小心, 足 in 足球) even when you rarely see them alone.
