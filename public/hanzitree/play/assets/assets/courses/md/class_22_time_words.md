# Say when — the verb stays put

The verb does not change for past or future. A time word does the work. This lesson is choosing **明天**, **后天**, **星期天**, **星期一**, **周末**, **下周**, **下个月**, **今天**, or **昨天**.

## Where it sits

Time often comes first, or right after the subject. Either is fine.

**今天我有课。** I have class today.

**我明天要考试。** I have an exam tomorrow.

If **要** is on the card, leave it — it is the plan word, not the blank.

## Which day

**昨天** yesterday. **今天** today. **明天** tomorrow. **后天** the day after tomorrow.

**星期天** Sunday. **星期一** Monday. **周末** the weekend. **下周** next week. **下个月** next month.

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill the blank with the time word.
