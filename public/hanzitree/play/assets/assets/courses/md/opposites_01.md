# Opposites

**Opposites** mode pairs antonyms side by side — **上/下**, **大/小**, **进/出** — with pinyin and English for both.

One character is missing. Your job: **assemble the opposite** from components, in the correct order.

## This set

The **most common** antonym pairs in everyday Chinese. When both words share a character (e.g. **进门/出门**), you only build the part that changes.

## Why it sticks

Opposites travel together in speech and reading. Learn one, and its partner is already half familiar.
