# I, you, and a simple sentence

Assemble one character from its parts. These are the small words every sentence needs.

**我 / 你 / 他 / 她 / 我们** are the people. **是** names a noun (**她是老师**). **很** sits before an adjective (**很好**).

**吗** at the end turns a statement into a yes/no question. **姓** is the family name (**我姓林**), not the same as **叫** for a given name.
