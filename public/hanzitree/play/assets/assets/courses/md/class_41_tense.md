# Mark when it happened

The verb stays the same. A small marker does the tense work. This lesson fills **在**, **了**, **过**, **要**, **会**, **前**, or **后**.

Do not fill the day-word (**明天** is the Time lesson). Skill **会** (*know how*) is Modals. Location **在** (*in the kitchen*) is Prepositions.

## 在 — was in the middle of it

A past time, then **在** before the verb.

**我当时在遛狗。** I was walking the dog at that time.

## 了 / 过

**了** is this time: it happened. **过** is experience: you have done it before.

**我遛了狗。** I walked the dog.

**我遛过狗。** I have walked a dog before.

## 要 / 会

**要** is a plan you intend. **会** is what will happen — often weather.

**我明天要考试。** I have an exam tomorrow. (plan)

**后天会下雨！** It will rain the day after tomorrow. (prediction)

## 前 / 后

Stick **前** or **后** on the end of the action. English says *before/after* first; Chinese says the action first.

**上学前** before going to school. **回家后** after going home.

Not **往前** (that is Towards). The English line on each card is the clue.
