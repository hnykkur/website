# Opposites III

**Opposites III** is the third antonym batch: same puzzle shape, **sharper vocabulary**.

## This set

**Less common but still useful** pairs — **买/卖**, **借/还**, **强/弱** — the kind you meet in stories, signs, and later lessons.

## The drill

Both sides stay visible; you only assemble the **non-shared** opposite character. Shared pieces (like **门** in **进门/出门**) stay put.
