# Nature pictographs

This node uses the **nature / environment** pictograph list: landscapes, weather, plants, and related **simple forms**.

## Linguistic note

Many of these characters started as **drawings of concrete things**. In modern Chinese they often appear as **parts of compounds** rather than alone—knowing the shape still anchors meaning when you meet new vocabulary.

## Why these shapes stick

**About 5%** of the **top 1,000** Chinese characters contain **at least one of these pictographs**.
