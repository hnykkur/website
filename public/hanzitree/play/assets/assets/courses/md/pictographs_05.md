# Pictographs V

This set collects **additional pictographic** characters from the wordlist that are not in pictographs **01–04** or the radical courses — sorted by frequency and Jun Da rank.

## Themes in this batch

Includes **direction, color, ritual, and nature** forms (e.g. 南, 云, 龙, 黄) plus smaller utility glyphs (止, 页, 贝).

## Recognition only

As with other pictograph nodes: match the **English meaning** to the correct **whole character**.
