# Opposites II

**Opposites II** keeps the same rhythm: two words in a row, one blank, **build the missing character**.

## This set

**Mid-frequency** pairs — **得/失**, **远/近**, **新/旧** — still common in conversation and graded readers.

## What you already know

Many targets reuse shapes from **Foundations**, **Radicals**, and **Opposites I**. The contrast is the new hook.
