# Radicals — first set

Chinese characters are built from smaller building blocks called radicals. A radical is a component within a character that often gives a clue about its meaning or category. Radicals act a little like roots in English words: by recognizing them, learners can begin to guess what unfamiliar characters are related to.

Traditionally, radicals were used to organize Chinese dictionaries, helping readers look up unknown characters. Today, they remain one of the most useful tools for learning Chinese. By understanding radicals, students can break complex characters into smaller, meaningful pieces and begin to see patterns throughout the writing system.
