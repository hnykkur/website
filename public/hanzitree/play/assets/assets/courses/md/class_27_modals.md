# A word before the verb

These little verbs sit in front of the main action. This lesson is choosing among **会**, **想**, **可以**, **能**, **要**, and **应该**.

## 会 — know how

**会** is a skill you learned. Not “will” — that is a later lesson.

**我会说中文。** I can speak Chinese.

## 想 / 要

**想** is *want to*. **要** is a plan: you are going to do it.

**我想养狗。** I want to raise a dog.

**我明天要考试。** I'm going to have an exam tomorrow.

## 可以 / 能 / 应该

**可以** is permission — may I? **能** is whether you are able, body or situation. **应该** is *should*.

**我们可以坐这里吗？** May we sit here?

**我能看见。** I can see it.

**你应该睡觉。** You should sleep.

Put **不** in front of the modal to negate. The English line on each card is the clue.
