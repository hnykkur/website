# The extra phrase sits before the verb

English often tucks *at*, *with*, *for* after the verb. Chinese puts that chunk **in front** of the action. This lesson is choosing **在**, **跟**, **给**, **从**, or **用**.

## 在 / 从

**在** is *at* / *in* a place (not “in the middle of doing”). **从** is *from* a place.

**她在厨房做饭。** She cooks in the kitchen.

**我从学校回家。** I go home from school.

## 跟 / 给 / 用

**跟** is *with* a person. **给** is *for* someone — and another verb still follows; this is not “to give.” **用** is *using* a tool.

**我跟朋友吃饭。** I eat with friends.

**我给你做饭。** I'll cook for you.

**我用筷子吃饭。** I eat using chopsticks.
