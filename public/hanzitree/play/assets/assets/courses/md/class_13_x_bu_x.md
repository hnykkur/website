# Yes or no, with 不 in the middle

Chinese can ask a yes/no question by saying the word, then **不**, then the word again. One answer fills both blanks.

## Tags at the end

**对不对？** Right? **是不是？** Is it? **好不好？** OK?

Stick one on a statement to check: **他是老师，对不对？** He's a teacher, right?

## Asking how someone is

Same shape with an adjective: **你饿不饿？** Are you hungry? **你忙不忙？** Are you busy?

## Asking about a verb

The object comes after the second copy: **你买不买咖啡？** Are you buying coffee?

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill both □ with the same word.
