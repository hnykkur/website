# What they wear

Fill the missing word. English tells you which. Most blanks are Clothes words; a few are **穿**, **洗**, **贵**, **便宜**, **新**, or **试**.

**我喜欢这顶帽子。** I like this hat. (**帽子**)
**我想试这件毛衣。** I want to try on this sweater. (**试**)
**我还要洗衣服。** I still need to wash the clothes. (**洗**)

Thirty everyday cards. One blank each.
