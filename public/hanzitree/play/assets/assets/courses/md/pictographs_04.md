# More pictographs

**More pictographs** adds **objects, places, and tools**: vehicles, doors, writing, height, work, and other **high-use** simple characters not covered in earlier pictograph sets.

## Mode reminder

**Pictographic** rounds are **translation → Hanzi** only — never composition.

## Why these shapes stick

Many of these appear in **compound words** you will meet in **Foundations IV** (门, 车, 文, 高, 时).
