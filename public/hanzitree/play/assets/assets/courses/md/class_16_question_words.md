# Words that ask

Question words stay in the same spot the answer would take. This lesson is choosing the missing one.

## 谁 / 什么 / 几 / 多少

**谁** who. **什么** what. **几** a small number (time, age, a few). **多少** how many or how much.

**现在几点？** What time is it?

## 哪 / 哪里 / 哪儿 — often the same “where”

**哪**, **哪里**, and **哪儿** all mean *where*, and they are often interchangeable.

**书在哪？** / **书在哪里？** / **书在哪儿？** Where is the book?

Any of those three counts here. Bare **哪** at the end of a question is *where*. **哪** in front of a noun is *which* — **哪个** which one, **哪国** which country — and that is not interchangeable with **哪里** or **哪儿**.

## 哪些 and 呢

**哪些** is *which* for more than one: **哪些菜？** which dishes?

**呢** tosses the same question back: **他呢？** And him?

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill the blank with the question word.
