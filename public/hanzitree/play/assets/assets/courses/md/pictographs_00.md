# Tree pictographs

This lesson introduces **木 tree** — one of the oldest pictographs in Chinese — and the **family of characters** built around it: roots and tips, groves and forests, rest under shade, fruit and harvest, and everyday objects made of wood.

## Linguistic note

**木** started as a simple drawing of a **tree with trunk and branches**. Many later characters add strokes **above or below** 木 (本 root, 末 tip), **repeat** it (林, 森), or **pair** it with another shape (休 person + tree, 果 field + tree). Recognizing this cluster makes new vocabulary easier to parse.

## Why these shapes stick

**About 4%** of the **top 1,000** Chinese characters contain **木** or were built directly from this tree pictograph.
