# Foundations

You are in **Foundations** mode: short words from the foundations list, with rounds that target **one character** at a time. Your task is to assemble a whole character from two or more components. Take care of the correct order for components.

## How it fits the skill tree

This set is meant to bridge **simple pictographs and radicals** and **real course vocabulary**. Many targets can be built only from shapes you have already met in earlier nodes.

## Why this set sticks

**About 5%** of the **thousand commonest** characters appear in **this lesson’s list alone**.
