# What you like to do

Fill the missing word. English tells you which. Sometimes that is **也**, **都**, **最**, **一起**, **再**, or **还**.

**我爸爸打篮球。** My dad plays basketball. (**篮球**)
**我妈妈也来。** My mom is also coming. (**也**)
**我们一起去游泳。** We go swimming together. (**一起**)

Twenty cards. One blank each, from Hobbies plus those sentence words.
