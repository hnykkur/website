# 往, then which way

**往** is the frame: toward a direction, then a verb. This lesson fills the one character after **往**.

## Left, right, forward, back

**左** left. **右** right. **前** forward / ahead. **后** back.

**往左转。** Turn left.

**往前看。** Look ahead.

## In, out, up, down

**里** inside. **外** outside. **上** up. **下** down.

**往里走！** Go inside!

These are motion after **往**, not “the park in front” (**前面**). Do not add **面**.

## Compass

**东** east. **南** south. **西** west. **北** north.

**往东走。** Go east.

The English line names the direction. **往** stays visible. Fill the blank with **左**, **右**, **前**, **后**, **里**, **外**, **上**, **下**, **东**, **南**, **西**, or **北**.
