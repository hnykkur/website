# Little words before the verb

Adverbs go in front of the verb — even in front of **是**. This lesson is choosing the missing one.

## 也 / 常常 / 不常

**也** *also*. **常常** *often*. **不常** *not often*.

**他也是老师。** He is also a teacher.

**她常常买咖啡。** She often buys coffee.

## 非常 / 比较 / 特别

These turn up the volume: **非常** really / extremely, **比较** relatively, **特别** especially.

**他非常喜欢跑步。** He really likes running.

**这个比较贵。** This is relatively expensive.

The English line on each card is the clue. Fill the blank with the adverb.
