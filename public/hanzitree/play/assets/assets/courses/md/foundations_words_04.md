# Foundations IV

**Foundations IV** is the fourth **foundations** composition set. Rounds still target **one character** at a time, assembled from known components.

## Multi-character rows

Some TSV rows are **words** (e.g. 文学, 时间). The trailing **digit** picks which character is tested (文学**1** → 文). Add separate rows if you want both glyphs drilled.

## What you already know

Shapes from **more pictographs**, **Radicals IV** (speech, movement, door variants), and all earlier allowlists apply.
