# Pictographs I

MuZi_Talking.png
Chinese writing began thousands of years ago with simple pictures called pictograms. A pictogram is a character that originally looked like the thing it represented. Early people carved these symbols onto bones, shells, and bronze objects to record ideas, events, and names. Over time, the drawings became more stylized and compact, eventually turning into the Chinese characters used today.

LaoLin_Reading.png
Although only a small percentage of modern Chinese characters are pure pictograms, these ancient picture-symbols formed the foundation of the entire writing system. Learning them helps students see that Chinese characters are not random shapes, but parts of a logical system built from meaning, imagery, and historical evolution.
