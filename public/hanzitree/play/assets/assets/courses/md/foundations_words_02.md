# Foundations II

**Foundations II** continues **word-mode composition**: one character per round, built from **two to four** components in the correct IDS order.

## What you already know

Targets draw on **nature pictographs** and **Radicals II** from the parallel skill-tree branch, plus earlier course glyphs.

## Why this set sticks

These are **high-frequency** words (Jun Da rank ≤ 251) that reuse shapes you have already practiced.
