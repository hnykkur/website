# Radicals — supplemental

This course covers **extra radical forms** from the Kangxi-style inventory that **Foundations IV** does not drill directly — variants and neighbors like 示, 犬, 糹, 竹, 貝, 鳥, and 革.

## When to use it

After **Radicals IV**, if you want broader **form recognition** before tackling rarer vocabulary.

## Mode

**Guess-the-radical** — pick the component that matches the English gloss.
