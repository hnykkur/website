# Radicals IV

**Radicals IV** adds radicals for **speech, spirit, heart, dog, head/page, square, dish, door, and walking** (訁, 礻, 忄, 犭, 頁, 辵, and related forms).

## Interaction

Same as earlier radical lessons: read the **meaning**, pick the matching **glyph form**.

## Payoff

These components appear throughout **Foundations IV** (们, 问, 这, 认) and general vocabulary.
