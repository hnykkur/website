# Expressing distance with 离

<!-- G34 · duo-draft · Duolingo Chinese · Screenshot_20260912_003539_Duolingo.jpg -->
To compare how far two places are, use **A + 离 + B**, then **远** (far), **近** (close), or a distance.

## Pattern

| Pattern | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A **离** B + 远 / 近 | A is far from / close to B |
| A **离** B + distance | A is [distance] from B |

## Examples

**商场离这里非常近。** — The mall is very close to here.

**故宫离这里一千米。** — The Forbidden City is one kilometer from here.
