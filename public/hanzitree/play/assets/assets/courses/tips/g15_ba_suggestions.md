# Making suggestions and offers with 吧

<!-- G15 · duo-draft · Duolingo Chinese · Screenshot_20260911_234912_Duolingo.jpg -->
Add **吧** (ba) at the end of a sentence to soften it into a suggestion or offer. The pronoun determines the meaning:

## Pattern

| Pattern | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 我们 + verb + 吧 | "Let's..." (suggestion) |
| 我 + verb + 吧 | "Let me..." (offer) |

## Examples

**我们去公园吧！** — Let's go to the park!

**我做晚饭吧！** — Let me make dinner!
