# Possessives with 的

<!-- G03 · duo-draft · Duolingo Chinese Section 1 Unit 5 · Screenshot_20260911_233102_Duolingo.jpg -->
To show possession, add **的** (de) after a pronoun. This works like adding 's or using words like "my" or "his" in English.

## Pronouns

| Pronoun | Possessive |
| --- | --- |
| 我 (I) | 我的 (my) |
| 你 (you) | 你的 (your) |
| 他 (he) | 他的 (his) |
| 她 (she) | 她的 (her) |

## Example

**你的妈妈** — your mom
