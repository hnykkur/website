# Expressing existence with 有

<!-- G32 · duo-draft · Duolingo Chinese · Screenshot_20260912_003227_Duolingo.jpg -->
To say something is **there** at a place, use **location + 有 + thing**. English "there is/are" does not map onto **是**.

Negate with **没有**, not 不有. You can put a quantity before the thing.

## Pattern

| Structure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Location + **有** + thing | There is/are … |
| Location + **没有** + thing | There is/are no … |

## Examples

**书房里有书架。** — There is a bookshelf in the study.

**客厅里有两个沙发。** — There are two sofas in the living room.

**小区外没有咖啡馆。** — There is no café outside the residential area.
